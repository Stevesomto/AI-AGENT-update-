Two AI agents, one collaborative problem-solving loop.

CoLearn AI is a proposed system, based on a generative-AI multi-agent research design, where a subject-matter Expert Agent and a process-focused Assistant Agent support students working through problems in groups — while giving lecturers a clear view of how each group is progressing.

Two agents, two jobs

Each agent has a distinct scope in the proposed design, so their responsibilities never overlap.

Expert Agent

Academic assistant for subject-matter guidance.

  • Answers students' academic questions
  • Explains difficult concepts and gives examples
  • Identifies knowledge gaps and recommends resources
  • Evaluates submitted solutions

Assistant Agent

Collaborative learning facilitator.

  • Monitors group participation and discussion activity
  • Flags students who may need help and unanswered questions
  • Encourages collaboration when discussion goes quiet
  • Helps the group stay focused on the problem
